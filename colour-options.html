Onyx & Crimson is now live — and seven more combinations to choose from

The colours come straight from the reference board supplied by the school: onyx black surfaces, the deep maroon mid-tone and a crimson accent (#e4002b), over off-white and light grey. It repaints the four portals, the public website and all eight home page designs. Any of the others can be swapped in the same way — preview live first, then apply. The colours below are the real values; nothing here is a mock-up.

Two ways to switch.
  1. Open the live picker: /colours on the running system — every tile there has a Preview and an Apply button, and applying writes the choice to the school server.
  2. Or from the admin console: Backend Customization Zone → Branding → Primary / Accent Colour, Sidebar Theme and the Quick Theme Presets (which carry these same combinations).
A preview never changes what visitors see until you press Apply — the preview bar always says so.
Also changed with this palette: the school logo is now a drawn crest (shield, open book and flame) in the same onyx and crimson, and the home page walks down the campus with alternating photographs — see logo-preview.html.
