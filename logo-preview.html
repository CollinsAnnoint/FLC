The school crest

A heraldic shield with a cross, a flame and an open book — faith and learning — drawn as vector art for the school in the palette of the reference board: onyx black, crimson, cream and grey. Original artwork, no third-party or traced imagery, so it stays crisp in the browser tab, the portal sidebar, on letterheads and on signage.
