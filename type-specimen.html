Faith Learning Centre · typography

The new type system

Baskerville for the display voice, Candara for everything you read at length, Consolas for the numbers a school lives by — admission numbers, paybill codes, marks. All three families ship with the operating system, so the site renders the same everywhere and nothing is fetched from a font service.

Faith Learning Centre runs a Ministry of Education registered Competency-Based Curriculum from Pre-Primary to Junior School. Every class is kept small enough for the teacher to know each learner by name, and every mark a teacher records reaches the parents' portal the same day.

Paragraphs, tables, forms, the portal sidebar, buttons and every label. Larger counters and wider spacing keep long passages comfortable on a phone as well as a desktop.
