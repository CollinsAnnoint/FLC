Seven colour combinations to choose from

Each combination repaints the whole system — the four portals, the public website, all eight home page designs, the documents and the login screens. Preview any of them live on the real website, then press Apply to make it the school's colour. Nothing changes until you apply.

How to change the colour later. Backend Customization Zone → Branding → Primary / Accent Colour and Sidebar Theme still let you mix any two colours by hand, and the Quick Theme Presets there apply these same combinations in one click. This page is simply the fastest way to compare them on the live site.
